## 1. Is the work compared with the state of the art?

**Yes.** Five representative state-of-the-art works are used as comparison points — four on the
counting side, with official or publicly available weights, and one on the vision-language side:

1. **Wang, B., Liu, H., Samaras, D., Nguyen, H.** *Distribution Matching for Crowd Counting* (DM-Count),
   NeurIPS 2020 — run with its **official weights** on our density-regression ladders, both in-domain
   and under a training-domain swap. *(Reference [1].)*
2. **Song, Q., Wang, C., Jiang, Z., et al.** *Rethinking Counting and Localization in Crowds: A Purely
   Point-Based Framework* (P2PNet), ICCV 2021 — **official weights**, on the identical item set,
   protocol and metric convention. *(Reference [2].)*
3. **Li, Y., Zhang, X., Chen, D.** *CSRNet: Dilated Convolutional Neural Network for Understanding the
   Highly Congested Scenes*, CVPR 2018 — used as the **implementation-defect control**: a public
   reproduction of this architecture is reported separately from the official-implementation
   comparison, precisely so that the defect is not counted as a paradigm difference. *(Reference [40].)*
4. **Ma, Z., Wei, X., Hong, X., et al.** *Bayesian Loss for Crowd Count Estimation with Point
   Supervision* (ICCV 2019) — density-regression baseline on the synthetic count-controlled and dense
   ladders. *(Reference [39].)*
5. **Bai, S., Cai, Y., Chen, R., et al.** *Qwen3-VL Technical Report* — one of the released
   vision-language models we serve locally and measure; the vision-language side additionally compares
   **Qwen2.5-VL-72B/7B**, **InternVL2.5-8B** and **InternVL3.5-38B**, and a locally served census of
   **fourteen configurations**, including **five quantisation builds of a single 32B checkpoint**
   (AWQ-4bit, AWQ-8bit, GPTQ-W4, FP8, BF16) plus a hosted panel. *(References [36], [38], [4].)*

Beyond these, the detection side is compared against **YOLOv12n** with and without in-domain weights,
**SAHI** tiling, **Faster R-CNN** and **RetinaNet** with COCO weights, on one task, domain, protocol and
item intersection.

## 2. Which public datasets were used?

All image corpora are **public**; none is redistributed by us (the release carries per-image records
keyed by file name, so every number can be recomputed against the original datasets):

| Role | Datasets |
|---|---|
| Dense crowds | **ShanghaiTech Part A and Part B**, **UCF-QNRF** |
| Aerial / tiny objects | **VisDrone-DET**, **AI-TOD** |
| Microscopy | **BBBC005** |
| Sparse and open-vocabulary counting | **SFCHD**, **FSC-147**, **TallyQA** |
| Source of pretrained detector weights only | **COCO** (used for the zero-shot detector setting, not as a counting benchmark) |
| Synthetic, generated by our own released script | count-controlled grid corpus (blur, arrangement and count manipulated orthogonally) |

Two disclosures, both stated in the manuscript:

* **CountBench is excluded from every reported number.** Our probe sends the image only, whereas the
  corpus query used for that dataset places the caption text in the prompt, so the condition asks a
  counting question about images that contain no countable instance of the queried category. We keep
  the raw records in the release but mark the condition ill-posed and do not draw conclusions from it.
* For the aerial domains, the `abstain` column of the legacy corpus tables is a **keyword proxy**
  (a zero prediction or one of a small set of phrases), not a contract-offered abstention; the paper
  reports it as such and does not treat it as an abstention channel.

## 3. Which validation metrics were used?

* **Signed error direction and magnitude.** The pooled relative deviation $\rho = \text{pred}/\text{gt} - 1$,
  reported under **both** conventions — abstentions counted as 0 and answered items only — with the
  propositions that show when and why the two must diverge.
* **Abstention.** The answered-zero rate (with the measured identification constant
  $\kappa = \#\{\text{zeros}\}/\#\{\text{textual refusals}\}$), the explicit-refusal rate, and the
  **abstention share $S$** of the aggregate under-count in closed form, whose empirical value is
  82–94% on the four dense and aerial domains under the base contract arm.
* **Detection ladders.** MAE, and the **$\tau$-span in percentage points** at which the sign of the
  pooled deviation flips, with the target count $M$ as the density covariate.
* **Legibility.** A per-image proxy with selector AUC, plus the direct manipulations (blur, resolution,
  occlusion, tiling).
* **Statistics.** Bootstrap confidence intervals, paired comparisons on the common item intersection,
  a **measured** noise floor — from repeated *service starts* on the true-zero pool (channel composition
  moves by at most 2.6 pp) and from a same-scale re-measurement of the public-benchmark panel (within
  0.5 pp in 9 of its 12 cells) — with the resulting "differences below this are not reported" rule,
  and 10,000× bootstrap plus exhaustive split-point enumeration where a structural claim is made.
